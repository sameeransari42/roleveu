# Roleveu 🚀

**Beyond Keywords. Find Your Right Fit.**

Roleveu is an AI-powered job portal that aims to connect job seekers with relevant opportunities through intelligent resume analysis and job matching.

### ✨ Key Features

- 📄 **Resume Analysis** — Extract and analyze relevant information from resumes.
- 🎯 **Intelligent Job Matching** — Compare resumes with job descriptions beyond exact keyword overlap.
- 📊 **Match Scores** — Help users understand how closely their profiles align with job requirements.
- 🧩 **Skill-Gap Insights** — Highlight skills that may be missing or not evident in a resume.
- 💼 **Job Discovery** — Explore job opportunities through a centralized platform.
- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Recommendations** — Use NLP and semantic similarity techniques to support more relevant job recommendations.

### 🎯 Project Goal

To build a user-friendly job portal that makes recruitment more informative, transparent, and efficient by combining full-stack development with AI-assisted resume matching.
